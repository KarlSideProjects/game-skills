## v28 Fusion Pixel Font and HardMode label

The v28 candidate uses the official [Fusion Pixel Font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font) 8px monospaced Traditional Chinese BDF, version `2026.09.25`. The project-side source is `analysis/remaining-graphic-labels/hud-font-research/fusion-pixel-8px-monospaced-zh_hant.bdf`; the bounded HUD list check found all 171/171 codepoints. Each advances 8px; 169 glyphs have BBX `7x7, x=0, y=-1`, and two have `6x7, x=1, y=-1`. With `FONT_ASCENT=7`, place rows at y=1 and x at the BBX x offset. The font is licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; preserve its copyright/license notices and the bundled upstream font notices when distributing derived font data. Coverage does not replace in-game visual acceptance.

The v28 HardMode label adapter appended four 8px glyphs (`困難模式`) after the preserved `ABS.TXT` area at FONT offset `0x3F0C8`; its build preserved the original 128-glyph ASCII atlas. The title label was visually confirmed only in a bounded diagnostic that forced one host-side RAM byte from `T` to `D` at 0.BIN offset `0x438FE` / CPU `0x060478FE`. The unforced v28 title path leaves the gate at `T`; the natural game path that changes it is unknown. Do not change this gate as a persistent patch or claim a normal HardMode menu path from the forced run. The evidence is `analysis/native-full-runtime-proof/movie-overlay-abi/support-hud-only/UI-audit/hard-mode/v28-hard-mode-runtime-proof.json` and the v5 private candidate component manifest. It does not prove every later-mode tile allocation or whole-game HUD coverage.

The v29 name/class result is independent of the v28 title-label diagnostic. The forced HardMode diagnostic still does not prove the natural game path that changes the gate, and the earlier four-glyph title pass does not expand the v29 battle proof beyond the tested visible name/class row.

## Movie evidence boundary

The private v25 movie candidate passed only the tested M0001 intervals with runtime overrides off: bottom captions at frames 1,000 and 1,500, a clear gap at 2,900, the next caption at 3,000, and the centered M0001-027 card visible at 7,900–8,400 then clear at 8,500. The overlay was checked across both alternating video surfaces (`0x25E00000`, `0x25E40000`, 1,024-byte row pitch). This is interval evidence for one movie, not runtime proof for all 100 captions or the other seven clips.

Movie helper cave space is candidate-specific. The private candidate places a 4 KiB pixel lookup table at FONT `0x3C000..0x3D000` and resident helpers in matching `0.BIN`/`1.BIN` range `0x76C0..0xBA48`, guarded by the native-font pointer targeting the cartridge font bank. It restores the source `MOVIEPRG` prefix at `0xB418`: audio initialization reads it at cold frame 211 (PC `0x06031DEE`, source `R4=0x06089E18`, destination sound RAM starts `0x25A00710`). Zero-filled bytes or lack of instruction fetch alone do not prove a region safe for reuse. Preserve the trace at `analysis/native-full-runtime-proof/movie-overlay-abi/factorial-movie-only/runtime-cold-sound-narrow/memory/sound-poll-write-regs.tsv` with any candidate evidence.

Use the project SH2Disasm to check loader prologues, branch targets, delay slots, literal pools, load base and source offsets. Preserve `FAA8` live `R0`, caller PR, stack and registers. Static selector and filename coverage are not a substitute for clip-by-clip runtime testing; do not assign unseen movie IDs by playback order.
